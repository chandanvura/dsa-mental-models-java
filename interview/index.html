Your answer flow when the code is not immediate

A structured explanation still proves that you can solve the problem. Speak while you reason.

  1. 1Clarify input, constraints, duplicates, and expected output.
  2. 2State brute force first and name its exact bottleneck.
  3. 3Name the pattern and the information you will maintain.
  4. 4Walk a small example with pointer/index movement.
  5. 5Write Java in small, testable blocks.
  6. 6Test empty, one-item, duplicate, and boundary cases.
  7. 7Finish with time and space—including recursion stack.
